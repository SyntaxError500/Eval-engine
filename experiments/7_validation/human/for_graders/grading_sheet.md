## A01

**Question:** Explain why a hot‑air balloon rises when the air inside it is heated. Answer in a short paragraph (3‑8 sentences).

**Answer:** When the air inside a hot‑air balloon is heated, its temperature rises and the air expands, which lowers its density compared to the cooler surrounding atmosphere. Because the heated air is less dense, the weight of the air displaced by the balloon is greater than the weight of the air inside it. This difference creates a net upward buoyant force, as described by Newton's third law. The surrounding air, being at ambient temperature, remains denser and thus pushes up on the balloon harder than the balloon's weight pulls it down, causing the balloon to rise.
